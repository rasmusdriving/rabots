# What's new in Pobots

Each version of Pobots, newest first. A Pobots that updates itself shows these notes before and after an update.

## 0.45.0 (21 Nov 2026)

- Before you update, Pobots shows what the new version brings, on the Pobots card in Settings and in the update dialog.
- After an update, Pobots tells you once what is new, on a card you can dismiss.
- The main bot can tell you what an update does, before and after.

## 0.44.0 (20 Nov 2026)

- Send files over a connection to another Pobots.
- Choose how long a connection lasts: 24 hours, 30 days or until you end it.
- Each connection belongs to one bot.

## 0.43.0 (20 Nov 2026)

- Bots tell you when a to-do gets its block of time in your calendar.

## 0.42.1 (20 Nov 2026)

- The switch for to-do blocks in Settings works again.

## 0.42.0 (20 Nov 2026)

- Ahead needs a calendar, and the main bot can set one up for you.
- Google Calendar works too, through a small script in your Google account.

## 0.41.1 (20 Nov 2026)

- Step-by-step guides for setting up the calendar and Teams connections.

## 0.41.0 (20 Nov 2026)

- To-dos can get a block of time in your calendar.
- After an update, the page reloads once by itself and keeps what you were typing.

## 0.40.0 (19 Nov 2026)

- Ahead has a new look, with a week grid of what is coming up.

## 0.39.0 (19 Nov 2026)

- A new Ahead screen shows what is coming up for you and your bots.

## 0.38.1 (19 Nov 2026)

- Downloaded files keep their own names.

## 0.38.0 (19 Nov 2026)

- Send a bot on vacation: it stays out of the way until it comes back.

## 0.37.1 (19 Nov 2026)

- Codex limits show next to the hub.

## 0.37.0 (19 Nov 2026)

- Connect two Pobots so their bots can talk to each other.

## 0.36.0 (18 Nov 2026)

- Backups to iCloud only copy what changed, so they are faster and smaller.
- Cards on the rail fade in and out calmly.
- A card can ask several questions at once.
- A step that fails says what it could not do.

## 0.35.0 (18 Nov 2026)

- A Usage page shows what your bots cost, the tokens they use and how close you are to your limits.

## 0.34.0 (18 Nov 2026)

- Pobots updates itself with one click.
- Pobots works without Codex installed.
