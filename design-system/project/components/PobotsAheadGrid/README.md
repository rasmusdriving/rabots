# Ahead: the Week grid

Week on the laptop and Day on the phone: a column per day where height is time. The grid shows exactly the working hours from Settings, Ahead and fills the room that is left in the window, so the whole working day of five days shows with no scrolling. An hour is never squeezed under 48px; only in a window too low for that do the hours scroll inside themselves.

Three things are told apart at a glance, by their ground and nothing else. A meeting is the person's own: a grey block, muted text, no face, not a button. A meeting marked free is hollow, so busy time reads as filled; where it has the room it says "Free" after its time, and its hint says "Marked free". A bot's thing rests on the soft accent with the bot's face and full ink. What waits on the person (a "your time" slot, a to-do) rests on the soft amber. No colour per kind, no borders: the hour lines are a background.

A block shows what its height and width allow: one line, then the time under it, then one quiet line of facts in a tall block. A title wraps at words only and is never broken inside a word; in a block too narrow for that it is one line that ends in an ellipsis, and the full title is in the hint. The time is shown whole or not at all, because the grid already tells the time. New or moved since the last visit is a small dot on the corner of the face, so it takes no room from the title. Things at the same time stand side by side, never on top of each other; a column with a bot's thing in it is the wider one. From the fourth at one time they fold into "+3", which opens the day as a list.

On top of each day a strip of four fixed lines (three on a phone) holds what has a day but no time, with "2 more" when there is more and "From earlier · 2" on today. The bots' things come first, and when the day has room each of them takes two lines, so its title can be read; an all-day meeting is always one line. One thin line above the hours and one below hold what lies outside the working hours, each with its time; they are there only in a week that has such things, and then for the whole visit. A day's name opens the day as a list in a sheet; the list scrolls inside the sheet.

Now is a thin line with a dot in today's column, drawn over the grid. Today's name is in the accent text; the column itself is not tinted.

Dragging: an open item or a "your time" slot goes to another day and time, snapped to 15 minutes. While it moves, an outline in the day says "Tue 15:15" and the block itself stays put, faded. Dropped on a day's strip, a plain item gets that day with no time. The old box stays, faded, and says where the item went. Escape lets go. A press that does not travel is a click and opens the sheet. Mouse and pen only: on a touch screen the sheet moves things.

Nothing jumps. Everything is placed in percent of the working day and nothing is measured in script. The strips and the lines have fixed heights whatever is in them. Sheets float.

On a phone the grid shows one day, 72px an hour so a quarter of an hour still holds a line, and the page scrolls. A strip of the working days on top switches the day.
