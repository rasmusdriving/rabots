The sheet for making a channel, opened by the add control beside "Channels" in the roster. A Dialog of the sheet size.

- One field: the name, in focus when the sheet opens. A hint says to use the channel's name in Teams, and that writers and tags are set on its page.
- "Create channel" waits for a name. Enter creates too. After creating, the channel's page opens in the reading panel and its row in the roster is the open one.
- A new channel is on, with nobody who may write, no tags and no address for answers, so nothing reaches a bot until those are set.
- Escape, "Cancel" or a click on the dimmed layer closes it; focus goes back to the add control.
