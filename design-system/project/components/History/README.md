History lists the saved changes to a bot's folder, newest first, and lets the person undo any of them.

You provide who made each change (you, the bot or the app), what in plain words, which files, and when.

- Who: the word "You" or "App" on a small `fill` disc, or the bot's own face.
- What: one plain line, "Edited the job", "Kaja changed 2 files", "Undid: Edited the job". The paths sit under it in mono.
- When: kind with time, "Just now", "12 min ago", "Yesterday 09:12".
- A row opens in place to show what changed. The diff sits in a well in mono. Added and removed lines are told apart by a soft tint (`diff-added`, `diff-removed`) and by a + or - sign, never by a border or a coloured stripe.
- "Undo this change" is a secondary button under the diff. It asks first and saves a new change that reverses it; when that cannot be done cleanly, it says so and changes nothing.
- More changes load as you scroll. Nothing reorders while you look.

## Earlier chats (step 7b, 14 Nov 2026)

Above the changes, "Earlier chats": the bot's chats by their titles, newest first, five and then **Show more**. Each row is a quiet word for its kind ("Chat" for a session of the chat with you, "Run" for a fresh run, "Helper" for a helper's work), the title on one line, and when it began. Rows are plain text, not controls. The changes follow under the heading "Changes to the folder". Titles are made by a small model when a chat ends or is set aside; a chat that gets its title shows at the top at once.
