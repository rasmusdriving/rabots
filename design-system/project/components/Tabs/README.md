Tabs are the quiet words in the head of a bot's page: Chat, Job, Files, Artifacts, Triggers, History, Settings.

You provide the words and which one is open.

- Tabs are words, never icons. Seven at most; a bot's page has seven (Triggers came in step 2b, Artifacts in step 2f, when the person asked for the bot's artifacts to have a place of their own). Do not add an eighth: put it inside one of the seven.
- The open tab is `ink` on a soft `fill`. The others are `ink-faint` and turn `ink` on hover. No underline, no border.
- They sit beside the bot's name and status. When the panel is narrow they move to their own line under the name, and on a narrower panel they sit a little closer together, and closer again on the narrowest (an artifact open beside the chat in a small window), so all seven always fit.
- On a phone the seven are wider than the screen: the row scrolls sideways inside itself, never the page. The open tab is always brought into view (at once when the page opens, calmly after a change), and the row's edge fades softly on the side where more tabs are, so Settings at the far end can be found (fix 0.26.3). The fade is a mask only; nothing moves.
- Chat is the default. The open tab is remembered per bot while the app is open.
- Keyboard: one tab stop for the group; Left and Right move between tabs, Home and End go to the ends.
- Leaving a tab with unsaved text asks first.
