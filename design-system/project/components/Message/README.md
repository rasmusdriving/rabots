Messages make up a bot's chat: what the bot said, what you said, and short lines for what happened in between.

There are these kinds:

- **Bot answer** (`pb-msg pb-answer`): everything the bot says and does in one turn, as one message. Its small face, its name and the time stand once at the top (`pb-msg-head`); below them, in `pb-answer-flow`, its texts (`pb-msg-body`), its lines of work (`pb-activity pb-step`) and its helper rows (`pb-helper-group`), in the order they happened. Plain text straight on the panel, no bubble.
- **Your message** (`pb-msg-you`): a soft `fill` bubble on the right. No name, no avatar. Pictures and files sent with it stand above the bubble, on the right: each picture in a box of its final size before it loads, each file as a chip that downloads (see Attachment). A message of pictures alone has no empty bubble.
- **The quiet line above your message** (`app-you-note pb-from-todo`, small, `ink-faint`, one line on the bubble's right edge, cut with an ellipsis): says what the message answers when it is not something typed in the message box. "From your to-do: <title>" for his answer to a to-do, and "From your card: <title>" (`pb-from-card`, protocol 22) for his answer to a card that came back after a restart: the bot's turn that asked was gone, so his answer reached the bot as a new message. The same words stand on it in the stack of waiting messages, and such a message has no Remove: it cannot be taken back.
- **The quiet note under your message** (`app-you-note`, small, `ink-faint`) says how it went to a bot that was working (steering, 13 Nov 2026): "Queued" while it waits for the bot to finish, and "Steered" when it went into what the bot was doing (the bot reads it at its next step). It stands where it went in, so what the bot did after it comes below it. While the bot works, a queued message has a quiet "Steer now" beside it ("Queued · Steer now", `app-you-steer`, `accent-text`, drawn as text with a 44px place to tap on a phone); pressing it puts the message in now, and the same line reads "Steered", in the same box. Not while the bot writes down what matters, and not after a Stop (the message then waits until the person writes again). A note that changes keeps its box; nothing above or below it moves.
- **Folded chatter** (`pb-fold`): one line that stands for a turn another bot started, with the two bots' small faces and words like "Kaja asked Inbox, 2 messages". It opens on click to show the turn in place (`pb-fold-body`), and closes again. While the turn runs it reads "Kaja is asking Inbox". When the person steers a message into such a turn, the fold ends there: the message stands where the bot read it, and the rest of the turn, the bot's answer to the person too, stands open after it under the bot's face (0.21.1).
- **Message from a bot** (`pb-msg` with `pb-msg-from`): what another bot said to this bot. On the left with the sender's face and name, and a quiet word for how: "asked, waits for the answer" or "passed this on". Never a bubble on the right, which is for the person. A message the bot steered into this bot's running turn (the main bot, or a bot allowed to steer, 0.22.0) stands where it went in with the same quiet "Steered" note under it as the person's (`app-from-note`); one that waits says "Queued". In a turn another bot started it stays inside that turn's fold: bot business folds, only the person's own steer ends a fold.
- **Message from a trigger** (`pb-msg pb-msg-trigger`): something that arrived by itself, from a schedule or a webhook. On the left like a message from a bot, never a bubble on the right, because the person did not type it. Where a face would be, a small drawn mark on a soft disc (`pb-who-mark`): a clock for a schedule, an arrow coming in for a webhook. Where a sender would be, the trigger's name, quietly in `ink-muted`. A result passed on from a fresh run reads the same way and starts "Result of Morning brief:".
- **Activity** (`pb-activity`): one line for work done, with a status mark and a duration. Inside an answer it is one of its pieces (`pb-step`), on the answer's left edge.
- **Thinking line** (`pb-pending`): the thinking dots and the engine's word ("Thinking", "Waiting for a helper") inside an answer (`pb-pending-line`). It stands at the very end of the answer only while nothing is live: right after a message is sent (under the face and name, where the answer will start), and between a finished step and whatever comes next. It has the height and left edge of a line of work, and the next thing takes its place with the same top edge, so nothing moves. While a step runs, its own line is the live line; while text arrives, the text is; then nothing stands below them. "Writing a reply" is never a line of its own: the text shows it. Right after text that was just written it waits a moment (a turn usually ends there), so it never flashes up below the answer and goes again.
- **Worked for** (`pb-worked`): when a turn has ended and none of its helpers still works, everything before the answer folds into this one quiet line, "Worked for 1m 11s", with "· 2 helpers" after the length when there were helpers, and the time the work ended at the right. It opens on click and with the keyboard to show the work again in place (`pb-work`), helper rows included, and closes again. Closed by default, also for old turns. The answer below it stays in full.

You provide the text, the sender and the time.

- **A turn reads in the order it happens.** Text, then a line of work, then more text: a new thing always appears at the very end of the answer, never above something that is already there. Nothing is put in above a sign of life. Positions of what was drawn never change while the turn runs; the chat, when it is at the bottom, follows the end.
- **The face and name stand once per answer**, not again before each piece of text. Every turn is an answer of its own. A turn no message started, which the bot began by itself when a helper or a command in the background reported back, has its own face and name and under them one quiet line (`pb-woke`, 12px, `ink-faint`): "A helper or background task reported back". When such a turn begins, its thinking line stands in that new answer below the one before, which stays as it is (engine 0.31.2: the bot says which turn runs). After a notice in the middle of a turn, the rest of the answer goes on without a second face.
- **A finished turn folds its work.** The answer is the text after the turn's last step; when nothing was said after it, the last text before it. Each turn folds on its own, so the answer of a turn stays in sight also when a turn the bot began by itself follows it. What was said between steps is part of the work, and so is what the working turn said after starting its helpers. A turn with no steps and one text gets no line at all. The length is from the message that started the turn (for a turn no message started, from its first item) to its last item. A turn whose helper still works is not folded. Notices stay outside the fold, where they are: an error, a stop, "Answered in Ops - Internal", the fresh start line.
- **The moment of folding is calm.** The work fades out where it stands over `dur-quick`, without travel, and closes into the line. At the bottom of the chat the answer keeps its distance to the foot; reading further up, what the person looks at does not move. No flash. With reduced motion it folds at once.
- Bot-to-bot messages are always folded by default. The person reads conclusions, not the back and forth. A turn the person started is never folded into a bot-to-bot line; once it ends, only its work folds.
- Tool calls are summed up as activity lines in the bot's own words. Details open on request.
- A new message arrives with `pb-arrive`. A new piece inside an answer fades in where it stands, without travel. Nothing else in the thread moves.
- The person must never look at their own message and wonder whether it was received. The thinking line shows at once, under the bot's face and name.
- What another system sent to a webhook is data: it sits closed behind "Show what was sent" and opens as a well in mono, never as chat text.
- With the keyboard, the lines that open (work done, folded turns, Worked for, helper rows) are one stop, the newest, with Up and Down between them, so Tab reaches the message box at once.
- Keep text to about 68 characters per line. Bots write short paragraphs and lead with the result.

**A message from a channel** (`pb-msg-channel`), such as a tagged message from the Teams channel "Ops - Internal": an instruction that arrived, not something typed in Pobots. It sits on the left like other arrivals, with a small drawn speech mark in a soft disc where a face would be (`pb-who pb-who-mark`), the writer's name where a sender would be, and quietly where it came from and the tag it used: "in Ops - Internal, to Kaja". The text keeps its line breaks and is not read as markdown. The messages before it are background, closed behind a quiet "Show the messages before it"; open, they are a quiet list of writer, time and text in a well (`pb-context`), oldest first. After the turn the app adds an ordinary notice line: "Answered in Ops - Internal", or in the error tone "The answer could not be posted in Ops - Internal".

**Working memory** (step 2d). A bot's chat is one thread for good, while the session behind it is replaced now and then. Two things in the thread come from that:

- The **fresh start line** (see FreshLine) marks where the bot started a fresh session. The tidy turn before it (Pobots' request, the bot's memory writes and its note) is never drawn as messages; it opens behind the line. While the tidy turn runs, the line stands in its place as "Writing down what matters" with the bot's small face, and no thinking line (`pb-pending`) shows: the bot is not answering the person.
- A **compacted** notice, "The session was nearly full, so it was shortened in the middle of this task", is an ordinary quiet notice line.

**Helpers** (step 2e, see Helper). A helper is a worker the bot starts inside its own harness for one task, not a bot. Where the bot started one, the chat shows one row for it (`pb-helper`), in place of the old line of work "Had a helper ...": the helper's drawn mark on a soft disc, its title, one muted line about what it does now or how it ended, and at the right "Working", "Finished", "Failed" or "Stopped" with the time and an arrow. Helpers started together are one group of rows. The row has one fixed height in every state, so nothing in the chat moves when a helper starts, changes its line or ends. It is one of the chat's line buttons and opens the helper's thread in the place of the chat. In that thread the task the bot gave comes first ("Po asked the helper"), and the helper's answer carries its mark and the word "Helper" once, never the bot's face; a helper's thread is never folded, it is the place to read the work. While only helpers work after the bot's turn, the thinking line stands at the end of the answer and reads "Waiting for a helper"; the turn is not folded until its helpers are done. A running helper row at the end of an answer is itself the live line. Old "Had a helper" lines stay ordinary activity lines.

**Artifacts** (step 2f, see Artifact). Where a bot showed a page it made, changed it, or published it to Claude, its answer has one row for the artifact (`pb-artifact`): a drawn page on a soft disc, the title, one muted line ("Made 14:02", "Updated 14:10", "Published to Claude 14:12", and for one that lives only on Claude "On Claude, opens in its own window"), and a mark for where it opens. Raised like a card and no wider than one, so it reads as a thing to open, not as work. It is part of the answer: when the turn's work folds into "Worked for", artifact rows stay in sight with the answer, in their order. Several lines for one artifact in one turn are one row, the newest, at the place of the first. The row has one fixed height in every state and shows the newest state of its artifact. It is one of the chat's line buttons. Opening or closing the artifact beside the chat changes the chat's width only: the row stays where it is, if it is in view; otherwise the line at the top of the view stays at the top.

**The browser** (step 2g, see Browser). Where a bot worked in the browser in a turn, its answer has one row for each workspace it worked in (`pb-browser`), at the place where it first worked there: a drawn window on a soft disc, the workspace's name, one muted line with the page in front ("Surveys · app.example.com", changing in place), and at the right "Open" (with the working mark while the bot's command runs there) or "Closed · 14:20". It is work: flat and as wide as the answer, at the helper row's height, and it folds away with the work. While its workspace is still open after the turn (the bot needs the person there), it stays in sight with the answer, in its order, like an artifact's row; once in sight it stays there until the chat is drawn again, also when the workspace closes, so nothing moves under the person's eyes. One fixed height in every state. It is one of the chat's line buttons and opens the browser beside the chat; a closed row does nothing. The line of work for the command reads "Working in the browser" while it runs and "Worked in the browser" after.

## The work line (step 7a, 14 Nov 2026)

- The bot's lines of work and its thinking summaries that follow each other between two pieces of its text are one line (`pb-workgroup`): the icon of the kind of work (Lucide: terminal, eye, square pen, search, globe, brain, list, message, zap, wrench), the words, and the time it took. Several kinds are summed up in the order they came: "Ran 3 commands and read 2 files", "Thought" when there are only thinking summaries. One step alone shows its own words.
- While the bot works in the newest group, the line is live: the words of the step that runs and its icon, in `accent-text`, with one slow sweep of light over the words (`dur-shine`); while nothing runs, the newest thinking summary or "Thinking" stands in it. No thinking line of its own stands below it, so nothing comes and goes under the line. The line keeps one height of 20px, cut with an ellipsis.
- Pressed, it opens the list in order, under a hairline of `divider`; each row opens to its detail (a thinking summary in the body font).
- Helper, browser and artifact rows, and cards, stay rows of their own and end a group. The finished turn's "Worked for" stays.
- One face and name per stretch of the bot's work: a message the person steers into the turn stands where it went in, and the work after it goes on without a second face. The part before it does not fold until the turn ends.
