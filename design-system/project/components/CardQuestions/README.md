Questions before he acts (protocol 23, 17 Nov 2026): the person asks a bot about one of its to-dos or question cards before he answers it. The person asked: "Skulle alltid vilja kunna ställa följdfrågor på sånna här kort så jag kan få klargörelse innan jag agerar".

It stands at the foot of every question card a bot raised (also a task manager's, and one that came back after a restart) and of every to-do's window, under their own buttons, so they never move. Not on approvals, notes, Pobots' own cards or a helper's card.

- **The thread**: his questions on the right in a `fill` bubble (13/20), as in the chat; the bot's answer under each in the card's own type (13/20, markdown). It scrolls inside a box of its own past 200 px on a card and 260 px in the to-do's window, with the newest in sight.
- **The waiting line**, in `ink-faint` 12/20, keeps one line's height: "Kaja is answering" while the bot's turn for it runs, "Kaja answers when it is free" while it waits in line, "No answer came" when the turn ended without a word or Pobots stopped meanwhile. The answer takes its place.
- **The box**: one line, "Ask about this before you act", and a small "Ask" button. Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a new line inside it; it never grows. 32 px on a card, 36 px in the to-do's window, 44 px on a phone. "Ask" waits while an answer is on its way: one question at a time. The box empties the moment he sends, so his words never stand twice; if the question is refused (the card closed meanwhile), they come back in the box with the reason under it in `clay` 12/16.
- **The to-do's window keeps its top** where it opened (near the top, not centred), so a question and its answer make it longer downward only.
- When the bot asks again after his question, the new words fade in where the old ones were, in the same card at the same place, with the thread under them.
- His question also stands in the bot's chat, marked "Question on your to-do: <title>" or "Question on your card: <title>" above the bubble (the `.pb-from-todo` line), and the answer is the bot's reply there. When his question went into the bot's running turn (its waiting ask_human returned it), the bubble carries "Steered" like any message sent while it worked, and the bot's answer to him stays in sight when the turn's work folds into "Worked for", together with the turn's last words.
- Nothing jumps: sending keeps the card's box; no border, no colour of its own; no animation but the calm arrival of the bot's new words.
