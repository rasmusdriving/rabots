# Ahead

The screen for what is coming: one agenda for the next 14 days, from the bots' items, the person's own, to-dos with a date, one-time schedules and, when the calendar is on, meetings. Open it from the Ahead row at the top of the bots list.

Keep it calm. No colour per kind and no badge: a kind is a quiet grey tag. Amber only where something waits on the person ("For you" on a to-do). A row is one line: the time (a range as 09:00-09:30) in the mono face, a small mark, the title, and the quiet facts at the right end, so rows line up down the day and a full day stays short. On a phone the facts go under the title. A bot's item carries its face; the person's own a plain disc; a meeting a small square and a quieter title.

"From earlier" sits above Today and holds only items whose day passed. Today always shows, with "Nothing today" when empty; other days only when they have something. Further out is "Later", folded, grouped by week and then by month.

Nothing jumps. When a bot changes something while the person looks, hold it behind "1 new · Show", which has one fixed place: the right end of the row above the days, where the since words stand. It never floats over the days. Show is never cut: when the place is too narrow, the words before it are. In the row above the days, what empties while the person looks keeps its box until they leave the screen (an amber count that is now nothing, the chip of their own items, a count with a digit less), so nothing beside it moves. A row the person acted on keeps its box until they leave the screen; done or dropped rows fade and strike through; a moved row fades and says "Moved to Monday 23 Nov".

Tapping an item opens the sheet: Done, Drop, Move to a picked day, Open again, the way to the bot's chat. The bot is told at its next turn.

## Working the screen

Above the days stands one row of fixed height, whatever it holds. From the left: "Carrying", which opens Overview; a small chip per bot with something on Ahead (its face, how much, and in amber how much waits on the person), and "You" for the person's own items; the main bot's week note cut to one line (the words "Week note" when there is no room for a line); and at the right end what changed since the last visit ("2 new, 1 moved" or "Nothing new"). A chip is also the filter: tap it to show only that bot's rows, tap it again for everyone. On a wide page the chips carry the bots' names.

Overview is the same in full, one tap away, in a sheet: the week note, the since line with its time, a line per bot with its counts in words ("3 on Ahead · 2 due this week · 1 waiting on you"), and the labels as small chips. A label picked there shows as a chip in the row, where a tap clears it.

A dot and a word ("New" or "Moved from Monday") mark a changed row. The dot stands in the gap beside the row's mark and takes no room, so every title starts on the same line.

The laptop switches between Agenda and Week, the phone between Agenda and Day. Week and Day are the grid: see Ahead: the Week grid.

Add opens a sheet for a line and a day, marked as added by you. A plain item's sheet uses the shared dialog and question box. Move to a suggested day or pick a date, mark Done, Drop, or Open again. The sheet floats above the list. In Agenda, Done this week stays folded until opened. Use neutral surfaces and the existing amber text only where something waits on the person.
