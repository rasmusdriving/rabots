# Ahead

The screen for what is coming: one agenda for the next 14 days, from the bots' items, the person's own, to-dos with a date, one-time schedules and, when the calendar is on, meetings. Open it from the Ahead row at the top of the bots list.

Keep it calm. No colour per kind and no badge: a kind is a quiet grey tag. Amber only where something waits on the person ("For you" on a to-do). A row is a grid of time, a small mark and the title with one quiet line under it, so rows line up down the day. A bot's item carries its face; the person's own a plain disc; a meeting a small square and a quieter title.

"From earlier" sits above Today and holds only items whose day passed. Today always shows, with "Nothing today" when empty; other days only when they have something. Further out is "Later", folded, grouped by week and then by month.

Nothing jumps. When a bot changes something while the person looks, hold it behind one floating line at the foot ("1 new · Show"). A row the person acted on keeps its box until they leave the screen; done or dropped rows fade and strike through; a moved row fades and says "Moved to Monday 23 Nov".

Tapping an item opens the sheet: Done, Drop, Move to a picked day, Open again, the way to the bot's chat. The bot is told at its next turn.

## Working the screen

Above the days, a quiet summary says what changed since the last visit. A dot and a word (“New” or “Moved from Monday”) carry the same information on a row. Changes that arrive during a visit wait behind Show, without moving the days. Carrying is the one block above the days and also the filter: a line per bot with something underway, due or waiting (a bot's ask for your time this week counts as waiting), and "You" for your own items. Choose a line to show only its rows, the same line again for everyone. Labels sit as small chips on the Carrying heading line and work the same way; they combine with the bot filter.

The laptop can switch between Agenda and Week. Week is five working days based on the person's settings. The source slot stays quiet while dragging a plain open item, and says where the item went after the drop. Meetings, dated to-dos, runs and your time are not draggable. The phone always uses Agenda.

Add opens a sheet for a line and a day, marked as added by you. A plain item's sheet uses the shared dialog and question box. Move to a suggested day or pick a date, mark Done, Drop, or Open again. The sheet floats above the list. Done this week stays folded until opened. Use neutral surfaces and the existing amber text only where something waits on the person.
