A choice list shows a few options where exactly one applies, each with its hint visible. Used for a bot's access level in its settings.

You provide the options, their hints and the chosen one.

- Use it when the hints matter for choosing. When they do not, use the segmented choice in Field.
- The chosen option sits on `accent-soft` and carries a small check, so colour is not the only sign.
- It is a radio group: one tab stop, Up and Down move the choice.
- The choice saves at once; "Saved" shows beside the heading for a moment.
