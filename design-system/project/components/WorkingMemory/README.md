Working memory is a section of a bot's Settings tab. It says, in words, how full the session behind the bot's one chat is, and lets the person ask for a fresh start now. It sits after "Model and effort" and before "Access": both are about how the bot thinks, and the permission sections stay together.

You provide the bot's name, its `context` (tokens, window, since, tidying) and its state.

- **One sentence** on the arrangement: "Po keeps one chat with you for good. What Po can keep in mind at once is limited, so at calm moments Po writes down what matters and starts fresh. This shows how much is in mind now. At 100% Po starts fresh by itself."
- **A plain line of facts** (`pb-memory-facts`) joined by a middle dot: "About 38% full · Since 11 Nov 14:52". The share is of the size at which the bot starts fresh by itself, in whole percent ("Less than 1% full" below one). Right after a fresh start: "Nothing in it yet · Since 11 Nov 15:03". With no session yet: "Not started yet". The date is always written out here, also for today.
- **No meter, no bar, no number to watch**, here or anywhere else. How full a session is is not something to keep an eye on, so it is words in Settings only. The roster and the header show nothing new; while the bot tidies, its roster line reads what the engine sends ("Writing down what matters").
- **"Start fresh now"**, a secondary button (`pb-memory-act`). It asks first: "Start fresh now?", "Po first writes down what matters, then its next message starts in a fresh session. The chat stays as it is.", "Start fresh" and "Cancel". When the engine has nothing to tidy, "There is nothing to tidy yet." shows where "Saved" shows. A refusal from the engine shows in the question, which stays open.
- **Off, with the reason beside it**, while the bot is in a turn ("Po is working. Try again when it is done."), waits on a card of its own ("Po is waiting for you. Try again once that is answered.") or is tidying ("Po is writing down what matters."). The button keeps its place in the keyboard order (`aria-disabled`, not `disabled`), so focus does not drop to the page when a fresh start begins, and the reason is its description.
- No settings for the timing. They are in the engine's config.
