A note card is a bot telling the person something they should see, with nothing to decide.

You provide the bot, a title and a sentence or two.

- The head is the bot's face and name with "tells you". The title says the news in a few words.
- One quiet "Got it" puts it away. There are no other buttons.
- Bots use notes sparingly. Everything else goes in the chat.
- It leaves like any answered card: it settles and the rail closes the gap.
